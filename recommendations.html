About me by Bonnie

Hello!!

My name is Bonnie and I created this website!

I play soccer, and I love camping and swimming at the beach (even though I'm deathly scared of sharks!!). I paint, and I play the viola, the guitar and a range of other instruments.

I'm a proud Aussie girl!! I love learning about nutrition, herbal medicines and botany, and I adore everlasting flowers!

I hope you have a wonderful day. Sending you all love xx
