Reader art wall: theme "Home"

23 October 2026Your Voice

Our first art wall theme is "Home". Home can be a place, a person, a smell, a song or a feeling. What does it mean to you?

Ideas to get you started

  • Draw your favourite corner, like your bed, a window seat or a tree you climb.
  • Make a collage of colours and textures that feel like home.
  • Photograph small details: a mug, your shoes by the door, the light through a curtain.
  • Paint a place you've lived, or a place you dream of living one day.
  • Design a map of everywhere that has ever felt like home.
  • Write a short poem and decorate the page.

Need a starting point? Try our Create page to make a mood board of photos and stickers, then save it as a picture.

Home isn't always a place. Sometimes it's a feeling.

How sharing will work

Rebella's art wall isn't open for sending in work just yet. We are setting it up carefully so it is safe for everyone. When it opens:

  • A parent or carer will need to say it's okay before you send anything.
  • Every piece is checked and approved by an adult editor before anything goes on the site.
  • We only ever show a first name or a made-up artist name. Never your full name, school, suburb or photos of your face.
  • There are no comments or likes. The wall is just for enjoying each other's work.

Keep yourself safe in your art

Before you share any picture, anywhere, check it doesn't show your address, school uniform or logo, street signs, your house number or anyone who hasn't said it's okay to be in it.

In the meantime, start making! Keep your piece somewhere safe, and keep an eye on Rebella for when the wall opens.
