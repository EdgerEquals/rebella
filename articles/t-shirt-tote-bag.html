Turn an old T-shirt into a tote bag in 20 minutes

23 October 2026Make It

That T-shirt you love but never wear? It can have a second life as a bag. No sewing machine, no needle, just scissors and a few knots.

You will need

  • An old T-shirt (cotton works best; ask before you cut anything that isn't yours)
  • Sharp fabric scissors
  • A ruler and a pen, chalk or marker
  • A bowl or plate to trace around

Steps

  1. Turn it inside out and lay it flat. Your knots will end up hidden on the inside.
  2. Cut off the sleeves, just inside the seams. The holes become the tops of your handles.
  3. Cut the neckline. Place a bowl over the neck, trace around it and cut. A bigger scoop makes a wider opening for the bag.
  4. Decide the depth. Draw a line across the bottom of the shirt where you want the base of the bag. Above this line it stays a bag; below it becomes fringe.
  5. Cut the fringe. Cut strips about 2 cm wide from the bottom hem up to your line, through both layers.
  6. Tie it closed. Tie each front strip to the back strip directly behind it with a double knot. Then tie each pair to the pair next door so there are no gaps.
  7. Turn it right side out, and that's your tote.
Every bag you make is one less shirt in the bin.

Make it yours

Decorate it with fabric paint or fabric markers, iron-on patches, or a stencil cut from cardboard. Or leave the original print on show: a faded band tee or a shirt from a school camp makes a tote with a story.

A T-shirt bag is stretchy, so it is perfect for library books, swimming gear or a picnic, but don't overload it with anything too heavy.
