Music Playground

Turn things, tap things, see what feels good.

Have a play

There's no right way to do this and nothing to get right. Turn the dial until it feels right, build a tiny band, mess with the sound and see where it takes you.

Every sound here is made live by your browser. No microphone, no recordings of you, and nothing is sent anywhere. Sound starts after your first tap.

This browser can't make sounds on a web page, so the instruments and effects are resting. The radio still works.

The radio

Settle the dial where it feels right. The order shuffles every visit. Radio music plays in YouTube's own little player, so the effects below can't touch it, and the playground can't hear it.

Play along with the radio

Plays along by tempo and key tags, not by listening.

Instruments

      Coming soon: a 16-step grid to write your own parts, and sending your band to a real MIDI keyboard.

      Effects rack

      Coming soon: a looper, sampler pads, stutter and chop, all made from the playground's own sound (never a microphone, never the radio).

      Dad's ladder

      A little demo of how Dad's music picks its pace. Move the sliders and the readout changes. On Rebella there are no messages, so you're the one setting the mood.
