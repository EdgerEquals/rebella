Music

Real music by local and independent artists, free to play and share.

Support local. Press play.

Every track here was made by a real, independent artist who chose to share it under an open licence. That means they've said it's okay for anyone to play it, as long as we say who made it. So we do, on every track.

We're starting close to home. 2 of these 6 tracks are by Australian artists, and 2 are by a composer from Perth. Finding local music you're allowed to share is harder than it sounds, so we'll keep looking and keep adding.

Press play on any track. Only one plays at a time, and nothing starts on its own.

Try the Music Playground

Make music? Get featured

Writing songs in your bedroom? Making beats on a laptop? Playing in the school band? We'd love to feature young local artists here one day.

It isn't open yet. When it is, a parent or carer will need to say okay first, and an adult editor will check every track, just like Your Voice. Please don't post your name, school or contact details anywhere to get featured. For now, just keep making.

Artist and licence links go to other websites, so check with a parent or carer before you open them. The music plays from Rebella itself, and pressing play doesn't send anything about you anywhere.
